# PNG weapon icon study 01

3 November 2026. Jarred rejected the SVG direction and requested PNG exploration. Timer 1 / Reliquary is selected. These are draft icon studies, not approved production assets.

Open index.html to compare painted gunmetal and graphic metal at 96, 64, 48 and 32 CSS pixels, try a selected-weapon block and switch the background for a transparency check. Six designs: plasma mortar, rail carbine, chainblade, frag charge, jump pack, tracked drone.

Both source PNGs are 1536×1024 with genuine transparency, generated using the built-in image tool. They reference the existing art/shared/weapons-sheet.png for identity, not style. The page displays the six cells using CSS background positioning; individual trimmed/exported icons remain future production work. PNGs carry their painted colours; the old SVG palette toggle does not recolour these.

Files: painted-sheet.png, graphic-sheet.png, index.html, check.cjs, checks.json and three viewport screenshots. Browser checks at 1200/390/320px: six cells; current-weapon selection; style/background toggles; both images loaded; no page errors or horizontal overflow. ## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: TRANSPARENT PNG weapon inventory icon sheet for GRIMSPIRE, gothic cyberpunk artillery game. Six separate isolated icons arranged in a strict even 3-column 2-row grid; identical cell sizes and generous transparent padding, no text, no labels, no backgrounds, no UI frames, no pedestals, no connecting elements. Every icon fully contained inside its own cell, fills about 75 percent width or height. Weapons face right in side profile or very restrained 3/4 perspective, silhouette first, simple broad masses and only a few large details. These will display at 40 to 64px, avoid fiddly decoration and miniature screws. Reference image shows existing WEAPON IDENTITIES and proportions only; make higher-quality non-pixel raster UI art instead of tracing pixel edges. TOP LEFT plasma mortar: chunky large-bore short barrel launcher, big cylinder toward front, dark metal stock and pistol grip, orange plasma window and muzzle. TOP MIDDLE rail carbine: slim long parallel rail barrel, squared stock, pistol grip, cool pale blue energy slot. TOP RIGHT chainblade: diagonal upward chainsaw sword, large coarse silver teeth, black blade housing, short handle, vivid magenta power cell near hilt. BOTTOM LEFT frag charge: squat segmented cylindrical high-tech grenade, dark shell, mechanical safety lever and pull ring, tiny orange charge light. BOTTOM MIDDLE jump pack: compact paired vertical thruster tanks, central harness bridge, wide exhaust nozzles and tiny controlled cyan exhaust, not a backpack for hiking. BOTTOM RIGHT tracked drone: squat low tracked remote explosive vehicle, broad tracks, angular armored body, short antenna and small amber sensor, NO gun turret. The whole set: cool charcoal gunmetal, restrained silver edges and category-colour illuminated components, selective tightly contained bloom, vague gothic industrial construction with arched reinforcement and subtle engraved metal. No medieval fantasy ornament, no gold filigree, no skull obsession. Silhouettes distinct, strong middle values readable against near-black UI; don't disappear into black. Professional cohesive polished game UI asset sheet, not a concept painting scene.

Painted modifier: beautifully hand-painted dimensional metal miniatures, sculpted broad bevels, controlled painterly shading, subtle material wear, readable cool-grey planes with dark recesses; premium illustrated inventory art, bold at tiny sizes, no pixel art or simple vector outlines.

Graphic modifier: bold hand-painted cutout silhouettes, three to four large tonal shapes per weapon, crisp cold-grey edges and charcoal bodies, exaggerated chunky proportions, cel-painted bevels and a few luminous insets. Richer than line pictograms; no continuous neon outline.
