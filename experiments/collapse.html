COLLAPSE SANDBOX

Get your squad up to the belfry gate at the top before the sump takes them. Killing the Hollow is optional.

Each phase, every trooper can move and take one action, in any order. Then the Hollow all throw at once.

Drag back from a trooper to aim. ◀ ▶ walk, ⤒ jumps. The jump pack is movement: it spends walking, not your shot. The stone is masonry: break what holds something up and it comes down.

Nerve: ! shaken (aim wobbles), ✦ bloodlust (next shot hits harder), ✕ broken (bolts next phase).

Throwaway test, 8 Nov. Keys: arrows, space, 1–4 weapons, Tab next trooper, Enter end phase.
