# Set pieces: next batches

Two batches, proposed 14 Nov 2026 (Claude, for Jarred to pick from). Nothing here is approved until Jarred marks it. Each piece gets 2–3 alternate versions, so a map rarely shows the same one twice.

The kit so far: towers, spires, crowns (K01–K04), bridges and piers (K05–K06), nave walls and fronts (K07–K08), jetty (K09), landmarks (K10–K13), diagonals (K14–K17), rock foundations (R01–R09), and prop-size set pieces (S01–S09). What's missing is **things that stack and overhang**: ledges above ledges, shelves you can shoot under, and pockets with a roof. That's what batch 1 is for.

## Batch 1: play-layer pieces that break up the line (K18–K27)

Every one adds at least one level above the ground line and one undercut or covered pocket. All are destructible like the rest of the kit.

| # | Piece | What it adds to play | Alternates |
|---|---|---|---|
| K18 | **Ossuary balconies** | Two or three balconies jutting off a wall face at different heights, with shelves to hide under | (a) bone-lined with skull corbels (b) iron cages on brackets (c) one balcony torn loose, hanging tilted on its chains |
| K19 | **Hanging cage** | A platform hung in mid-air from an arch or crane, with cover inside and open sky under it | (a) bell cage (b) reliquary box, one side open (c) great censer bowl, swinging slightly |
| K20 | **Collapsed floor** | A building cutaway where a floor has dropped at an angle: a ramp up, a shelf over, a crawlspace under | (a) slab tilted one way (b) two floors pancaked with a gap between (c) floor snapped in the middle into a V |
| K21 | **Leaning tower** | A tower leaning 15–20° out over the water: its top juts, its base is an undercut | (a) lancet tower (b) belfry with the bell still in it (c) propped up by a fallen buttress |
| K22 | **Open spiral stair** | A tower with its wall gone, showing stair landings stepping up the inside | (a) newel post with stubbed steps (b) three landings, the middle one gone (c) stair wrapped round the outside of a pillar |
| K23 | **Aqueduct tiers** | Two stacked arcades: a walkable channel on top, arches below, water falling from the breaks | (a) both tiers whole (b) top tier broken mid-span (c) a three-tier stub |

| K26 | **Rib vault bay** | Free-standing vault ribs carrying a broken ceiling slab: a roof to stand on and a sheltered floor under it | (a) one bay (b) two bays, the middle one fallen (c) vault collapsed to one side into a wedge |
| K27 | **Capsized barge** | An iron hull half-sunk: a sloped deck, a hollow inside, the bow jutting up out of the water | (a) keel-up hull, walkable along the top (b) bow up at 30° (c) broken in two with a gap |

**If I had to pick three to start:** K20 collapsed floor, K21 leaning tower and K26 rib vault bay. They're the cheapest way to get shelves and undercuts onto every map, and they read instantly at game scale.

**Notes for drawing:** draw at kit density on the kit's grid, with the Body + Height layers like K01–K17. Mark placement data (see `WORLD-ART.md`): most of these want a `ground` or `cliff` socket; K19 wants an `under-arch` or `crane` hang point; K27 is `waterline`. Undercuts must be real gaps in the mask (at least 30 px clear so a trooper fits), not painted shadow.

## Batch 2: landmark levels

Pieces so big they *are* the map: the generator builds the shores and scatter around one colossal thing. Done as a big batch so no single one gets tired. Each is a set of modules (say 3–6 big pieces) rather than one image, so it can break apart and vary.

|---|---|---|---|

**Rules for landmark maps:** one landmark per map, never two. Roughly 1 map in 6, rolled from the seed like the other layouts. Shores and scatter still come from the generator, so it never plays the same twice. Faction spawns spread through the landmark's levels, as now.

**If I had to pick three to start:** L02 cathedral (you already want it), L03 fallen colossus (a bridge map changes how the water plays) and L05 organ (the strongest silhouette).
